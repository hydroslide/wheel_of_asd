Interpolation Details

3 Level 5 colors were interpolated based on L4→L5 progression patterns:

  • Emotional Regulation L5: #fdd800 - Saturated golden yellow (from bright yellow pattern)
  • Speech L5: #f94100 - Deep red-orange (from orange progression pattern)
  • Executive Functions L5: #57027b - Deep saturated purple (from purple progression pattern)

Method: Analyzed RGB progression patterns from 9 complete L4→L5 transitions and applied similar intensity changes.

Result: Complete 60-color palette with natural progression patterns maintained across all traits.
